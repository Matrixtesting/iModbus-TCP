iModbus-TCP Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

iModbus-TCP is a Modbus TCP client for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your iPhone except the Modbus traffic you send to your own devices.

Information we collect

None. iModbus-TCP does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves the devices and windows you set up (names, IP addresses, ports, register ranges, row names and formats) and your settings. This information is stored only on your iPhone. It is included in your iPhone's backups (for example, iCloud Backup) like other app data, and it is deleted when you delete the app. The developer never receives it.

Local network access

iModbus-TCP asks for permission to access your local network so it can connect to the Modbus TCP devices you add. It only connects to the addresses you enter, and only when you start polling or send a write. It does not scan your network or connect to the internet.

Children

iModbus-TCP is a tool for industrial equipment and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
